Access & Authentication
Admin Panel
You have a manual override system built into your admin panel. Any new user who signs up via Clerk authentication is by default in a pending state — they cannot access the platform until they are enabled. There are two ways a user gets enabled:

Manually — you or your team go into the admin panel and flip a switch to enable specific users. This is for your internal team members and testers who you want to give free access to during development.
Automatically — the moment a user completes payment via Stripe, their account is instantly activated. No manual step needed. Stripe sends a webhook to your backend, your backend flips the user to active, and they are in.

This gives you full control during early stage while still being completely hands-off once you launch publicly.

Pricing Model — Customisable Plan
Your pricing is not a fixed tiered plan. Instead the user builds their own plan by choosing exactly what they need. Here is how the pricing screen works:

Base Plan — $10/month
This is mandatory for all users and covers everything AI-powered:

AI priority ranking of all messages
AI message headlines
AI auto-drafted replies
Universal semantic search
Contextual assistant (cross-platform message linking)
Morning briefing and time-aware home screen
Unlimited Slack accounts included — Slack is free because you are using Slack's official API directly at no per-account cost to you


Add-On Accounts — $7 per account/month
Below the base plan, the user sees a section listing every other supported platform with an icon. Next to each platform is a plus and minus button. The user clicks plus to add an account, minus to remove one. Each account they add costs $7/month and gets added to their total in real time as they adjust.

Why This Model is Smart

Low barrier to entry — $10 gets them in the door with full AI features and Slack
Grows with the user — as their business grows and they add more accounts, your revenue per user grows naturally
No wasted tiers — users only pay for exactly what they use
Your costs are covered — at $7 per connected account you are comfortably covering your Unipile cost of $5.50 per account and keeping healthy margin on top
Slack as a free hook — giving Slack free is a smart acquisition move since many business users live in Slack already
